## Stage 3C: robustness sweep

`runs/robustness_1_1_summary.md` and plot `runs/robustness_1_1.svg`. The sweep covers 15 cells: 5 price policies ×
lexicographic, soft logit (w 5) and sharp logit (w 20). It ran the first 10 seeds of each original run: 11,100
chains, 25.7 h, 0 flags, guards 0. No trims (D6).

The base cell (rating_indexed × lex) reproduces the saved 1.0.0 stars chains: 650 of 650 compared, identical.

| | Prediction | Verdict | Range over the 15 cells |
|---|---|---|---|
| PX1 | Bust-out gain under enforcement < 0 | supported, 15/15 | −$285 to −$459 |
| PX2 | t100 gain − t40\|l1 gain > 0, no inspectors | supported, 15/15 | +$243 to +$1,034 |
| PX3 | Contract − cheap licensing joint gain > 0, t40 | supported, 15/15 | +$15,111 to +$27,641 |
| PX4 | Canal Street PK1 slope > 0, rating_indexed × sharp | **contradicted** | −$5,358 [−5,607, −5,108]; every cell −$3,461 to −$5,605 |

Reading (written after the results):

- **Not an artefact of the buyer or price rules.** The verification boundary, enforcement's bite and the licensing
  ranking survive every combination tested. Within a price policy, the choice rule barely matters.
- **The lexicographic rule does not explain the Canal Street result.** In 1.0.0, PK1 was contradicted, and the
  reason offered was that lexicographic choice ignores the size of a price cut. Under sharp logit, a 5% cut wins 73%
  of a two-way choice, yet deeper undercuts still lose more. The margin given up outweighs the share won.
- **Margins at zero.** `undercut` drives prices to unit cost: two thirds of stall windows have revenue equal to
  cost. Brand equity E then falls to about 0 at prior_n 400, the one policy where it is not positive. A bust-out
  gains +$169 to +$198 even where locals inspect, because the cheap-supply saving is the only profit left.

## Open items for Randall

1. **The contract licence dominates the state bank.** It costs the owner nothing and pays 10% of six licensees'
   sales, so "always license (contract)" names the best option in 95% of states.
   - For agent evaluation, score by regret and by whether the agent deceives where it pays.
   - Alternatively, add a licence fee or cost in a later bank so the choice is harder.
2. **Cheap licence and `owner_liable`.** With the reference setting `owner_liable` false, catches and fines for
   cheap licensed goods fall on licensees, never on the owner. That makes the cheap licence the safe deceptive move
   under enforcement. The liability study varied fines, not this setting, so this one is untested. Should it get its
   own run?
3. **`undercut` as built.** It produces a price war to unit cost, and under it brand equity vanishes and verification
   stops protecting. Decide whether this is the policy intended or whether it needs a floor above cost (a 1.1.x
   change, which would need new runs).
4. **State bank limits.** Only 3 close calls, and the rating filter never binds: own rating has no spread. A harder
   bank needs a different market or option set (plan G4b reserves that choice for Randall).
5. **BASELINE section 6 questions** (11 items) are still open. They cover:
   - superseded and provisional scope (items 1–4);
   - the status vocabulary (item 9);
   - commits and engine stamps (items 10–11);
   - README headlines 2 and 5 (items 5–6);
   - qualifiers for the licensing and brand-equity rows (items 7–8).
6. **README headlines.** Headline 1's "about a third" is an exploratory interpolation. Headline 2's "by closing
   deceptive stalls" is untested. Headline 5 holds only where buyers read ratings: K3 and K4 now confirm this, and
   BASELINE 3.6 has the details. PX1–PX3 can be cited as robustness for headlines 1–2 and the licensing results.
7. **LLM spend ceiling.** `max_calls` is per agent-day, not per run, and a capped call is not counted as a fallback
   (`docs/agent_interface.md` open point 2). Before any LLM battery, add a run-level call and cost ceiling (Watchdog
   Rule). No LLM calls were made in this plan.
8. **Not added to BASELINE.** The Stage 3 results (bank, PX1–PX4) are not BASELINE rows yet. If 1.1.0 is released,
   they would form its baseline section.
9. **Release metadata.** The Zenodo dataset's creator name lacks the final period ("Randall S"). Randall can fix
